## Correctness and integration argument

For a fixed closed node extent, substituting its preferred completed interior
preserves outside/ancestor extents. The first differing scoped descendant remains
decisive; later contexts cannot append inside its closed parent occurrence.
Concat therefore chooses the greatest feasible first-child end, alt its first
admitting child, and repeat its preferred complete child-interval sequence before
descendant choices. First empty participation beats absence; mandatory empties
remain and redundant optional tails lose. This is not a rank for unfinished
histories; the earlier strict-prefix reversal counterexample remains valid.

Membership keys are finite node/start/end/count tuples. Finite repeat counts are
0..255; unbounded counts saturate at minimum+1. The general rooted bound is
quadratic in subject positions times syntax/repeat states, not linear memory.
Selected tuples reference completed cells rather than copying histories. Full
proof/integration notes and all individual observations remain in the archive.

Before production, replace recursion with admitted explicit dependencies, measure
actual layouts using Unsafe.SizeOf, enforce conservative major growth before
allocation, and define useful depth/work behavior. Preserve strict/assumed UTF8,
byte boundaries, original interruption/first denial/refunds and cursor/result
lifetimes. Avoid a whole-subject coordinate pass on trusted early matches.
Native SKU/root costs need public comparison; current admitted DFS stays until
its duties are actually replaced. No dormant engine, framework, refcount/collector
or compatibility switch is introduced. The public shape/class grammar and actual
