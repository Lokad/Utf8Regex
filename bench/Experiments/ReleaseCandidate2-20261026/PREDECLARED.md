# RC2 actual-package/cumulative cost predeclaration

One bounded comparison of final RC2 package-extracted Core/PythonRe/PCRE2 DLLs
against the frozen correctness-equivalent 844dcbad binaries, not an intermediate
cleanup predecessor. Both are loaded by the same existing alternating ALC host.
All selected outputs/coordinates are checked before timing; eager outputs remain
retained. Original corpus assertions and detached fixture suites are separate.

Nineteen public/control lanes: Core compiled identifiers with unlimited/all-four
finite/live-token/absolute-deadline policies; finite scalar NonBacktracking;
Python captured Count and eager UTF-8 tuples; PCRE2 Unicode Count/enumeration and
dense/short replacement. Unchanged Core literal plus predecoded/strict-decode BCL
controls remain visible. All four finite controls: 50M work, depth1024, working64MiB,
result128MiB. These are experiment allowances, not recommended defaults.

Default runtime/tiering; existing least-contended single-processor scope. At least
2 s and 10,000 warm calls per version/lane; short replacement uses5 million warm calls.
Per-version calibration toward40 ms;9 alternating samples per lane. Inner 180 s cap,
outer 210 s. No builds/tests or concurrent benchmark jobs during measurement.
Raw samples, allocations and all loaded identities are retained.

Accept the cumulative public-cost gate only with correctness-equivalent outputs
and no demonstrated >10% regression. Controls/spreads must support interpretation;
uncertainty is not a pass. At most one reversed-first17-pair confirmation for
uncertain rows; then stop. Do not tune, repeat a benchmark campaign, refresh old
snapshots, weaken resource admission or repack immutable RC2 bytes.
